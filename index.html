Building a local lead list from Google Maps: field notes

These are working notes from prospecting small local businesses for B2B services (web design, local SEO, bookkeeping).

Why Google Maps

Maps listings are the most current public record of which local businesses exist, what category they claim, and how customers rate them. The missing piece is usually an email address.

Export step

I search one category and one city, then export the results with a Google Maps email extractor (I settled on Google Maps Email Extractor after comparing a few Chrome extensions). It pages through results, removes duplicates, and pulls a contact email from the business website when one is published, alongside phone, address, rating and social profiles. Export is CSV, XLSX or JSON.

Before outreach

  • Verify every address.
  • Remove chains and closed listings.
  • Write a first line that references the listing itself.
  • Honour opt-outs.
